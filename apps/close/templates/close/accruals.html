How accruals are worked out

Rules, in the order they apply

Received, not booked. Invoices and credit notes ShipMatch has received that belong to the period (dated on or before {{ period|date:"j M Y" }}, or for a shipment that shipped by then) and are not posted to QuickBooks with a bill date in the period. They are accrued at their own amount.

Not yet invoiced. Shipments that shipped on or before {{ period|date:"j M Y" }} (on-board date on the bill of lading, else its issue date), within {{ report.settings.lookback_days }} days, and are missing charges that Month-end settings expect: {{ expected_text }}. Each missing group is estimated from the quote on file, else the vendor's median on the same lane and equipment, else your median per container, and shows its confidence.

Not accrued. Rejected shipments, possible duplicate invoices, shipments without a shipping date, older shipments and charges someone marked as not needed are listed above instead.

The live report uses everything ShipMatch knows today, so invoices that arrived after the period end replace estimates. Locking stores the report as a version that never changes; locking again creates the next version.
