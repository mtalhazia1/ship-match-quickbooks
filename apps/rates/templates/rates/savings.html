How these numbers are counted

Caught is every amount a check flagged before payment: charges above the quote, extra charges nobody approved, duplicate invoices, totals that don't add up and unusual amounts.

Prevented: the shipment was rejected, or the invoice was corrected, replaced or taken out so the check no longer finds the problem. If a corrected invoice is still too high, only the reduction counts.

Still at risk: the issue is open on a shipment nobody has approved or rejected yet.

Accepted: a reviewer accepted the warning or an approver overrode the error, or the shipment was approved with the warning open. The charge was paid as invoiced.

Money flagged by two checks on one invoice counts once. A catch that goes away because a quote or rule changed is not counted. Each catch belongs to the month it was first flagged.
