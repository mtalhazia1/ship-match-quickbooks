How this is worked out

  • Hours saved per month = invoices per month × (minutes per invoice today − minutes with ShipMatch) ÷ 60.
  • Labor saved per year = hours saved per month × 12 × loaded hourly cost.
  • Overcharges caught per year = invoices per month × 12 × share with an overcharge × average overcharge.
  • Net benefit per year = labor saved + overcharges caught − 12 months of ShipMatch.
  • Payback = a year of ShipMatch ÷ one month of savings: how many months of savings pay for the year.

Assumptions: every overcharge caught is either not paid or credited back by the vendor; time saved is time the team can spend on other work. Not included: setup time, savings from fewer late-payment fees, or the cost of overcharges you would have found anyway. All figures come from the numbers entered above.
