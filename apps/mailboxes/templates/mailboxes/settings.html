Forward from Outlook or Microsoft 365

  1. In Outlook on the web, open Settings, then Mail, then Rules, and add a new rule.
  2. As the condition, pick your suppliers' addresses or “Has attachment”.
  3. As the action, pick “Redirect to” and paste the address above. Save.

If Outlook says forwarding outside your company is blocked, ask your Microsoft 365 admin to allow it for this address, or connect the mailbox directly under Connect a mailbox.

Forward from Gmail

  1. In Gmail, open Settings, See all settings, Forwarding and POP/IMAP, and add the address above as a forwarding address.
  2. Gmail sends a confirmation code to it. The code shows under Recent emails on this page; enter it in Gmail.
  3. Create a filter, for example from:harborlink.com has:attachment, and choose “Forward it to” this address.

You can also forward any single email by hand.
