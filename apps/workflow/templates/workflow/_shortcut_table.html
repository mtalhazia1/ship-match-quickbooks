Keyboard shortcuts
PressTo
j kMove to the next or previous row in a list. On a shipment, open the next or previous shipment in its tab.
EnterOpen the selected row
xTick or untick the selected row in the review queue
aApprove. A confirmation opens first; nothing is approved until you confirm.
rReject. Opens the box for the reason.
eEdit the selected value on a shipment or document (the first value to check if none is selected)
g then qGo to the review queue
g then dGo to documents
g then sGo to savings
/Search shipments and documents
?Show this list
EscClose a dialog or menu

Shortcuts never fire while you type in a box. Hold no other key with them (Ctrl, Alt and Cmd keep their usual meaning).
