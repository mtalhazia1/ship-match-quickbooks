Landed cost

How freight, duties, insurance and other charges are spread over the products on a shipment's commercial invoices. Reviewers can choose differently for one shipment. Approved shipments keep the landed cost worked out when they were approved.

By value suits most goods. By weight or volume suits freight on heavy or bulky goods, when the commercial invoice prints weights or volumes per line.

For a type of charge

For example freight by volume and duties by value. Default uses the choice above, unless the charge's source suggests a basis (customs duties are assessed on value).
