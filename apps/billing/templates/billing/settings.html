How billing works

  • Payments, cards and invoices are handled by Stripe. ShipMatch never sees card numbers.
  • Switching plans takes effect at once; Stripe charges or credits the difference.
  • If you cancel, ShipMatch keeps working until the end of the paid month. After that new documents pause, and everything already in stays available.
